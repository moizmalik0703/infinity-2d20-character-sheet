Custom House Rules — ability bonuses (v81)

Roll a minimum of 2d20 and a maximum of 5d20, including Momentum and Heat dice. Difficulty is the number of required successes (minimum D0). Reroll each eligible die at most once and accept the replacement. Heat Boost: add 1 Heat to the GM pool per explicitly permitted extra d20 before rolling. Heat Reroll: add 1 Heat per explicitly permitted reroll after rolling. Heat does not replace Momentum unless an ability says so.

Use only the highest applicable numerical rank within a progression; earlier acquired skill grants and unlocked capabilities remain. Selecting an approved higher rank represents its acquired prerequisites, which the player and GM must confirm. The sheet includes fixed lower-rank skill grants once, and offers choices where the catalogue grants a choice of subskill. Main skills cannot use Focus or receive automatic ability Rating bonuses. Acrobatics is an Agility subskill. Situational effects are reference text, not automatically applied to a dice roll.

All playable Trait and Talent mechanics are Custom House Rules. The 152 approved workbook entries override prior versions; existing supplemental entries retain their custom effects and require GM adjudication where their lifepath or source terminology differs. Twelve new faction/shipboard entries are marked for playtesting.

Legacy saves: if you previously manually allocated Talent or camouflage skill grants as starting bonuses, remove those manual duplicates after importing. Back up an XML copy before editing.
