# Campaign Roadmap After Session 7
## GM PLANNING — NOT PLAYED CANON

Session 7 demonstrated that PALE can insert MIRRORGLASS copies before Team Bravo arrives and can now mask signatures well enough to undermine the team's previous screening assumptions.

The campaign therefore shifts away from repeatedly asking whether a single person has been replaced. The remaining arc should expose the **infrastructure** behind the replacement network.

### Session 8 — THROUGH THE DAYBREAK GATE / OPERATION GATEFALL
Purpose: seize the live Daybreak logistics node; learn what PALE gained at Concilium; expose the supply route.

Planned answers:
- Daybreak was reactivated using apparently legitimate authority derived from compromised Council identities.
- Most personnel at Daybreak are genuine Ariadnians following orders they believe are lawful.
- The Concilium operation doubled as a live validation test for MIRRORGLASS signature masking.
- Daybreak is a relay, not the ultimate source.
- The next physical route points to an orbital transfer node provisionally designated KESTREL-9.

### Session 9 — ORBITAL INTERCEPTION
Purpose: pursue the supply chain off Dawn and identify the production/control infrastructure behind MIRRORGLASS.

Do not lock the exact villain or PALE origin before Session 8 is played. Let the Session 8 outcome determine whether Session 9 is a boarding action, pursuit, rescue, or infiltration.

### Session 10 — FRACTURE PROTOCOL
Purpose: final response to PALE's attempt to turn identity compromise into Human Sphere-wide political fracture.

Keep the final location and PALE reveal flexible until Session 8–9 table decisions establish what the players actually uncover.
