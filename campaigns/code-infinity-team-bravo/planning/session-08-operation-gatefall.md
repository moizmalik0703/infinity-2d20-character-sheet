# Session 8 — Through the Daybreak Gate
## Operation GATEFALL
### GM PLANNING — NOT PLAYED CANON

**Location:** Daybreak Gate, Dawn  
**Expected duration:** 4.5–5 hours  
**Active roster assumed:** Rabbit (Team Leader), Gypsy, Buzzkill, Anya, Dr Farid Qassim “Q”, Tom Hendry “Jobe”  
**Session mix:** Action 45% / Roleplay and command 35% / Investigation 20%

## Core premise
Daybreak Gate has come back online under apparently valid Council authority. Team Bravo arrives with less than an hour before an unidentified orbital transfer completes.

The session begins **in motion**, with Team Bravo already seventeen minutes from Daybreak. The first Bureau Noir briefing provides three objectives:
1. Seize Daybreak Gate.
2. Keep genuine Ariadnan personnel alive.
3. Stop the active transfer and preserve the route.

## GM truth
- Most Daybreak personnel are genuine Ariadnians following orders that appear lawful.
- The reactivation order was issued before the Concilium attack using authority associated with compromised Council principals.
- Four MIRRORGLASS infiltrators are embedded in the reactivation detachment.
- The three biological seed consignments found in the Concilium ledger came through Daybreak from orbital node KESTREL-9.
- Concilium was also a field-validation test: PALE confirmed its new masking could survive Q's live screening and the team's known identity checks.
- This is what PALE meant by “I have what I need.”
- A final inbound cargo contains a Combined Army-derived morphogenic fabrication core modified with human systems.
- KESTREL-9 is the next lead, not proof of PALE's true origin.

## Run order
0. **The Gate Is Already Open** — strong cold open; deadline and player roles.
1. **Valid Orders** — genuine Ariadnan garrison blocks access under compromised but valid authority; MIRRORGLASS cell reveals itself.
2. **Three Loads In, One Signal Out** — freight-yard fight; establish that the seed consignments came from orbit and current traffic points to KESTREL-9.
3. **What PALE Needed** — control-bunker reveal; deliver the validation-trial truth automatically.
4. **The Last Transfer** — countdown finale around the incoming fabrication core.
5. **Beyond Dawn** — Daybreak secured; KESTREL-9 begins moving; Bureau Noir authorises pursuit.

## GM-friendly running rules
- Essential information is automatic.
- Use one meaningful optional test per scene unless players create a new plan.
- Failure changes time, position or cost — it does not block the story.
- Repeat NPC motives, enemy stats and scene objectives where used.
- Use the Transfer Clock as the only global tracker.
- Do not make the team search the site room-by-room; they always know the next meaningful location.
- End each scene on an explicit trigger that launches the next.

## Transfer Clock
Start at 6. Reduce only when the team loses meaningful time, triggers an avoidable alarm, or chooses a deliberately slow approach. Scene 4 normally starts at 4.

## Key NPCs
- **Major Elena Markova** — genuine Ariadnan officer commanding the emergency reactivation detachment.
- **Sergeant Kellan Ward** — genuine checkpoint NCO.
- **Pavel Rusk** — MIRRORGLASS logistics adjutant and local cell coordinator.
- **Daybreak garrison** — approximately 18 technicians, mechanics, infantry and medical personnel; most are genuine.

## Required Session 8 answers
1. Daybreak is a relay, not the source.
2. Compromised Council authority was used to reactivate it.
3. PALE used Concilium as a live test of MIRRORGLASS masking.
4. The new masking can defeat Team Bravo's previous scan assumptions.
5. The physical logistics chain points to KESTREL-9.
6. PALE remains unidentified.
