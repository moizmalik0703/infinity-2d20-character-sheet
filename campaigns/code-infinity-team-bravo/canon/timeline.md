# Canonical Timeline

## Session 1 — Operation Faultline
Baraka Deep-7, Dawn. A staged Ariadnan–Haqqislam conflict concealed non-human interference, the death/replacement of Captain Qasim, Rudenko's compromise and the marker **PALE/DAWN/07**.

## Session 2 — Operation Cinder Trace
The trail moved through the Gates of Dawn logistics network. Team Bravo recovered non-human biological material, identified Arachne-compatible PALE infrastructure and uncovered the route toward SABLE, Casablanca Sam and PROJECT MIRRORGLASS.

## Session 3 — Operation Dead Thread
At Gates of Dawn, the team encountered the wider MIRRORGLASS operation. Evidence established that MIRRORGLASS transforms a biological host into a specific human identity rather than creating a conventional clone from nothing.

## Session 4 — Operation Ashen Silk
SHIFA research and the Compatibility Matrix showed how MIRRORGLASS could be made predictable and scalable by starting with a target identity and finding a suitable host. The JADE KNIFE lead emerged.

## Session 5 — Operation Jade Knife
At Avalon, Team Bravo prevented a MIRRORGLASS false-Fiday operation targeting Yu Jing official Shen Bao. Gypsy was critically injured. Buzzkill encountered PALE directly. The operation eventually pointed toward BLACK LOTUS and ORIZON-4.

## Session 6 — Operation Watchglass
At ORIZON-4, BLACK LOTUS was revealed as an ALEPH-built O-12 safety programme designed to detect dangerous AI or anomalous digital evolution. PALE compromised NYSA through a backdoor previously introduced by Miriam Solano through BLACK MIRROR. Team Bravo stopped ORIZON-4 from being de-orbited into Dawn. The recovered forecast pointed to the Council of KNIVES at Concilium.

## Session 7 — Operation Knife Edge
Team Bravo supported the secret Council of Knives summit at Concilium. MIRRORGLASS security copies attacked after an EMT grenade disabled the heavy guards. Apparent principals Nadya Orlova, Simon Blumfeld, Charlton Branson and Margaret Fuhler escaped with the infiltrators, but the real Orlova arrived late, proving the summit had already been penetrated before Team Bravo arrived. Rabbit, Gypsy, Buzzkill and Anya pursued and destroyed the escaping copies. Q and Tom Hendry located the genuine Blumfeld, Branson and Fuhler in suspended animation inside a hidden MIRRORGLASS bunker beneath Concilium. PALE contacted Tom and Q, claimed to have what it needed, and referenced Daybreak. All eight genuine principals survived. Post-operation evidence showed PALE had begun masking MIRRORGLASS signatures and that three biological seed consignments had passed through DAYBREAK GATE, which had resumed communications.
