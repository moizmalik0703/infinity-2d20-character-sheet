# Unresolved Threads

These are active questions **after Session 8 — Operation DAYBREAK**. The three stated Session 8 objectives were achieved; the following matters remain open.

1. **Who or what is PALE/07?** Its true identity, origin, architecture, controller and endgame remain unknown.
2. **Where is Pavel Rusk?** The Daybreak logistics officer disclosed his PALE affiliation, escaped and is the subject of a Bureau Noir manhunt. Who else assisted him?
3. **What is the preserved biological entity?** One living fragment of the adaptive flesh-metal organism survived. Bureau Toth analysis is pending; its capacity to assimilate machinery and unleash psychic attacks demands investigation.
4. **What is the full biological supply chain?** Team Bravo confirmed an intercepted shipment from **Kestrel-9 to Daybreak Gate, Dock 7**, fulfilling the tertiary objective. Earlier records identified three biological seed consignments through Daybreak. The source beyond Kestrel-9, intended onward destinations and other consignments remain unconfirmed.
5. **What did PALE obtain at Concilium?** It previously told Q and Tom Hendry that it had what it needed; the relationship to the Daybreak entity is not yet established.
6. **How does PALE control MIRRORGLASS agents?** A PALE-linked relay was found beneath the destroyed checkpoint turret. Was it controlling, coordinating or merely communicating with the agents?
7. **How reliable are MIRRORGLASS scans?** Rusk's scan was inconclusive, while PALE has stolen Bureau Toth information capable of masking infiltration signatures.
8. **How far has the MIRRORGLASS network spread?** Concilium and Daybreak demonstrate penetration of high-security organisations and logistics facilities.
9. **How does PALE relate to BLACK MIRROR, BLACK LOTUS and possible Combined Army activity?** BLACK LOTUS is the ALEPH-built O-12 safeguard previously identified at ORIZON-4; the full relationship remains unresolved.
10. **Can Daybreak's records expose further shipments and collaborators?** The intercepted vessel's known origin and destination are established, but the intended final receiving party is unknown.
11. **Tom Hendry's administrative obligation.** His prior Ariadnan Cross of Valour has now been awarded. The required substance-abuse management workshop must still be completed **within six weeks** of its Session 8 confirmation.
