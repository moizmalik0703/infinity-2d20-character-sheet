# Session 8 — Operation DAYBREAK

**Campaign:** Code Infinity — Team Bravo  
**Theatre:** Daybreak Gate, Dawn  
**Personnel deployed:** Gypsy (field section leader), Tom Hendry (“Jobe” / “Joby”), Buzzkill and Anya  
**Personnel remaining at Concilium:** Dr Farid Qassim (“Q”) and Sergeant Major Rabbit  
**Classification:** O-12 RESTRICTED — BUREAU NOIR AFTER-ACTION RECORD  
**Canon status:** Played Session 8; supersedes conflicting pre-session plans

## Mission Briefing

In the aftermath of Operation KNIFE EDGE, Bureau Noir received indications that PALE/07's next move would involve Daybreak Gate. The hostile intelligence had already demonstrated its ability to infiltrate the Council of Knives at Concilium through MIRRORGLASS replacements and to conceal those infiltrators from established screening procedures. The recovered freight records also showed that three consignments of biological seeds had moved through Daybreak Gate.

Team Bravo was ordered to seize control of the installation and intercept an incoming vessel believed to be carrying a potentially hostile foreign entity. Gypsy, Tom Hendry (known as Jobe or Joby), Buzzkill and Anya deployed. Dr Farid Qassim (“Q”) and Sergeant Major Rabbit remained at Concilium to assist with the recovery and security operations following Session 7. Gypsy assumed section leadership for the Daybreak mission. The precise nature of PALE/07's intended cargo and the full extent of its plans remained unknown.

## Scene 1 — Trouble at the Checkpoint

Team Bravo boarded an O-12 shuttle, which Gypsy piloted at remarkable speed. After making excellent time, the operatives transferred to an Ariadnan armoured tactical vehicle for the final approach to Daybreak Gate. The installation was under heightened security: every arriving vehicle was undergoing a comprehensive inspection, leaving a formidable traffic backlog at the gantry. Armed personnel, defensive turrets and airborne drones watched over the checkpoint.

### Personal Observation — Buzzkill

This traffic jam is taking far too long. What's the holdup? Naturally, our fearless section leader, Gypsy, decides the appropriate solution is to start honking the horn. I say *fearless* because he's only recently been cleared of his insubordination charges, following favourable recommendations and evidence of his supposed personal growth. Apparently, patience wasn't part of the rehabilitation programme.

The horn attracts several very angry Ariadnan guards, and Gypsy immediately gets into a shouting match with them. I attempt to defuse the situation by identifying us as O-12 operatives on an urgent mission from Concilium. Their response is to draw their weapons, swing searchlights onto our vehicle and bring heavy defensive firepower to bear. As though we've just announced that we're the enemy.

This is the last time I'm helping this team.

### Personal Observation — Joby

We're surrounded. I count seventeen Ariadnan security personnel, two turrets and two drone-copters. Not ideal odds for a misunderstanding. I'm looking for the section commander when a woman emerges from the ranks, inspects the vehicle and checks each of us against her datapad. She stops when she gets to me.

Crap. Do I know her?

Then she calls out my name. Apparently, she's a fan—and she's astonished to find me alive, given the reports of my death. Ah yes. Rumours of my death have been greatly exaggerated.

I vouch for the team, and she relaxes enough to explain the problem. Daybreak received two conflicting messages from Concilium. One ordered the garrison to help the arriving O-12 team secure the facility. The other instructed them to deny us entry, detain us and shoot if we resisted. I ask Buzzkill to examine the transmissions. He decrypts them and demonstrates that one directive is a PALE/07 forgery. Someone has been interfering with Daybreak's orders.

### Personal Observation — Anya

Just as the argument seems to be ending, Buzzkill's device registers a cluster of small incoming contacts. We search the sky, but there's nothing overhead. Then I catch a glint of metal on the ground.

Bugs. Shiny things, like oversized cockroaches, scuttling beneath the vehicles around us. Big enough to notice if you know where to look. They're moving straight through the checkpoint.

**“KILL THE BUGS!”** I scream.

Too late. They detonate almost simultaneously. Vehicles erupt into flames, guards are thrown across the road and people are killed or badly wounded. Before we can even take stock of the damage, gunfire breaks out from the surrounding defensive positions. The checkpoint has become a battlefield.

## Scene 2 — The False Defenders

The explosions devastated the vehicle inspection area. Several Ariadnan guards near the vehicles were killed, with more seriously injured. Personnel in the elevated towers and fortified positions escaped the blasts, but then turned their weapons on Team Bravo and their own Ariadnan comrades. Buzzkill rescued the security commander and identified the attackers as MIRRORGLASS operatives. Team Bravo began defending the genuine security personnel, evacuating the wounded and attempting to suppress the compromised turret emplacements.

### Personal Observation — Gypsy

Okay. Friendlies are shooting at us. Buzzkill says they're MIRRORGLASS agents. What the hell are those? I've been away on leave, people! Just forty-eight hours ago, I died, got brought back and ended up with half my body reconstructed with SILK. Haqqislamite medical technology is amazing.

Anyway, back to the present. I'm the section leader. I need to step up. Those distant turrets are making life miserable for everyone, and they really need to go away. If only there were something I could do—

Wait. What's this I see?

### Personal Observation — Joby

The firefight is getting worse, so I decide to do what I do best: quietly get close to a turret position and dispatch the rogue agent operating it. Simple plan. Except nobody mentioned that these bastards are difficult to kill.

Also, I really should stop drinking, because I could swear the agent's hand just transformed into a blade. For a moment I think I'm imagining it. Then the son of a bitch stabs me three times with his blade-hand. Three times! So much for that theory.

Now I'm pissed. I return the favour and blow him to pieces. I turn towards the turret—and everything goes black.

When I wake up, I'm on the ground, surrounded by rubble. The turret isn't firing anymore. In fact, the turret and most of its tower have disappeared. I look across the battlefield and see Gypsy holding a fucking Panzerfaust, smoke trailing from the barrel, waving at me. Thanks, Gypsy. Really appreciate it.

### Relay destroyed and allies secured

With the turret tower destroyed, the MIRRORGLASS agents suddenly ceased their coordinated attack and appeared to vanish from the engagement. Anya found the wreckage of a transmission dish beneath the ruined tower, bearing a PALE/07 signature. She reasoned that the relay may have allowed PALE/07 to command or coordinate its infiltrators; destroying it could explain their abrupt disappearance. This remained an operational assessment rather than a confirmed account of the technology.

The surviving commander identified herself as **Lieutenant Commander Elena Markova**, Daybreak's chief security officer. Once briefed on the falsified directives and the MIRRORGLASS incursion, Markova accepted Team Bravo's authority and agreed to help intercept the incoming shipment. She escorted the team into the secured installation, arranged for replacement ammunition and equipment, and facilitated access to Daybreak's control systems.

## Scene 3 — The Cryogenic Trap

At the control centre, Buzzkill identified the approaching vessel as a dropship from **Kestrel-9**, a small lunar base near Dawn. It was scheduled to land at **Dock 7 in approximately ten minutes**. Team Bravo agreed to capture its cargo rather than destroy it outright, placing the shipment in deep-freeze containment until Bureau Noir and Bureau Toth could retrieve and examine it safely. Lieutenant Commander Markova assigned Daybreak logistics officer **Pavel Rusk** to help prepare a suitable cryogenic chamber.

### Personal Observation — Buzzkill

I don't trust this Rusk fellow. I run a MIRRORGLASS scan, but the results are inconclusive. Very troubling. Still, I can't start accusing people without proof. I've been wrong before. But this guy is shifty as fuck.

He reluctantly takes us to the facility's largest cryogenic chamber. Then he locks us all inside. Over the intercom, he proudly announces that he's working for PALE and that we'll never stop him or PALE/07. The bastard has the nerve to gloat.

Now supercooled gas is flooding the chamber. By my calculation, we have about two minutes before we freeze to death. I knew it. I bloody knew it.

### Personal Observation — Anya

Shit. We're trapped. There isn't an accessible control panel, and the heavy chamber doors sit flush with their frames. We're going to have to force our way out.

I get my jacks beneath the door and start prying open a gap. Everyone joins in, pushing and levering with whatever strength they have left. The temperature is falling fast. For a moment I think the door won't move. Then the frame starts to give.

We keep pushing until the whole thing breaks loose. We scramble into safety just before the chamber reaches lethal temperatures. That was far too close.

### PALE/07's challenge

Team Bravo reached the dock control room as the dropship entered Dawn's atmosphere and began its landing sequence. Buzzkill attempted to interfere with the approach through the flight-control system, but PALE/07 countered the intrusion and openly mocked the operatives for arriving **two minutes too late** once again. It declared that they would never stop its plans and challenged them to confront it at Dock 7. With the landing already underway, Team Bravo rushed to the dock.

## Scene 4 — The Abomination at Dock 7

Team Bravo reached Dock 7 in time to witness the dropship land. Its cargo was a small, coffin-like containment vessel. When the container opened, the team glimpsed a grotesque amalgamation of living flesh and metallic supports, apparently adapting and reshaping itself to suit the surrounding environment.

The screens around the dock lit up with PALE/07's image. The hostile intelligence warned its operatives that Team Bravo was approaching. At the far end of the landing area, Pavel Rusk was directing the transfer of the containment vessel onto an armoured transport. The entity was being moved, but its intended onward destination was not established.

### Personal Observation — Gypsy

Holy SHIT. What is that thing in the container? That's one ugly piece of shit.

Right, team, we need a plan. Joby, get up that crane and shoot anything hostile that moves. Buzzkill and Anya, find the relay towers and disable them. And see that big TAG? Take it out too.

As for me? I'm going to knock on the door.

### Personal Observation — Buzzkill

Our dear leader has instructed me to deal with an enormous TAG while he goes off to knock on a door. Oh, wait. Never mind. He's just pulled out his Panzerfaust and fired it straight into the armoured transport.

The vehicle explodes. Debris everywhere.

Right. I suppose that's one way to start the party.

### Machine and flesh

The fighting at Dock 7 was fierce, but Team Bravo gradually gained the advantage by disabling the TAG and destroying the communications relays that supported the MIRRORGLASS force. Then Pavel Rusk initiated an unknown process. The entity extended its living tissue into the wreckage of the armoured transport and the damaged TAG, pulling machinery into itself and reshaping the whole mass into a monstrous fusion of metal and flesh. The resulting abomination brought heavy weapons to bear against the team.

### Personal Observation — Joby

I really should stop drinking. What the FUCK did I just see? A lump of alien flesh just ate the remains of a tank and a TAG and turned itself into some kind of super-TAG!

Oh, shit. It can see me. It's trying to bring down the crane, and I'm a hundred feet up. If this thing knocks the crane over, I'm dead. I need to shoot it down before I fall to my death.

### The entity neutralised

The transformed entity unleashed devastating firepower and a psychic assault that incapacitated Anya and badly wounded other operatives. Gypsy and Joby concentrated their attacks on the creature's armour, gradually stripping away its mechanical defences and destroying the bulk of its body. When the abomination finally collapsed, a small piece of living biological material remained. Team Bravo secured and isolated this surviving sample as evidence for Bureau Toth.

The hostile transfer was stopped, and the team retained control of the immediate situation at Daybreak Gate. However, Pavel Rusk escaped during the incident.

## Bureau Noir Secretariat — Post-Operation Assessment

Bureau Noir received an urgent request from Concilium for assistance at Daybreak Gate. Elements of the Ariadnan Mountain Division and O-12 Security Forces were dispatched in response to the suspected MIRRORGLASS incursion and possible Combined Army involvement. Both forces arrived to find Team Bravo controlling the immediate tactical situation. The hostile biological-mechanical entity had been destroyed apart from an isolated living sample, which was designated for transfer to Bureau Toth for specialist analysis.

The investigation established a documented incoming supply leg from **Kestrel-9 to Daybreak Gate, Dock 7** and confirmed that the entity was being transferred into the Daybreak logistics network. This satisfied the team's stated tertiary objective of determining the origin/destination of the intercepted supply movement; the intended onward destination, full extent of the biological supply chain and purpose of PALE/07's broader operation remain unknown. Earlier evidence also documented three biological seed consignments passing through Daybreak Gate.

Pavel Rusk, identified during the operation as an agent acting for PALE/07, remains at large. Bureau Noir has established a manhunt coordination centre and directed Team Bravo to pursue and apprehend him. The identity and ultimate objective of PALE/07, the reliability of MIRRORGLASS detection and the nature of the entity's biological, mechanical and psychic capabilities remain matters of high intelligence priority.
